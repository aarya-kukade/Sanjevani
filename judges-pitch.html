The Problem we're solving

01

In a medical emergency, every minute counts. Yet patients and ambulance drivers in India have no fast, reliable way to know which nearby hospital actually has a free bed of the right type.

Wasted Time

Families call hospitals one by one — only to find no ICU beds, no emergency capacity, or wrong specialty.

No Visibility

There is no consumer-friendly map of nearby hospitals with bed-type availability shown together.

Wrong Triage

Patients often don't know whether their symptoms need General, Emergency or ICU care — and head to the wrong department.

Our Solution

02

Sanjivani is a single web app that combines real hospital location data, AI-powered symptom triage, and bed-type filtering — so any patient can find the right care in three taps.

G

GPS-First Discovery

One-tap access to your location. We auto-fetch hospitals within 10 km — and expand to 25 km if results are sparse.

M

Real OpenStreetMap Data

Hospitals come from live OSM (Overpass + Nominatim APIs) — not a stale local list. Works for any city worldwide.

AI

Gemini Symptom Triage

Type symptoms → Google Gemini suggests General / Emergency / ICU and offers a safe home remedy when appropriate.

B

Bed-Type Filtering

One click filters the entire map for the bed type the AI recommended — patients don't have to guess.

L

Live-Feel Updates

Bed counts refresh every 30 seconds, mimicking the real-time feel hospital networks should provide.

D

One-Tap Directions

Every card has direct Google Maps "Directions" and "View Details" links — no copy-pasting addresses.

Engineering Highlights

  • Fallback chain for reliability — Overpass fails? Auto-fall back to Nominatim. Gemini 429s? Exponential-backoff retry across 3 model tiers.
  • Strict triage prompt — the AI is constrained to return JSON-only, normalized to General / Emergency / ICU, with a safer default.
  • Pure-static frontend — works without a build step; deployable on any static host.
  • Backend-only secrets path defined — production roadmap moves the Gemini key out of the client.
