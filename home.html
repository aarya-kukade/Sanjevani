About Sanjivani

Sanjivani is a revolutionary healthcare management platform designed to solve one of the most critical problems in modern healthcare: bed availability and patient allocation.

By combining AI-powered symptom analysis, real-time GPS tracking, and a global network of partner hospitals, we ensure that every patient gets the care they need, when they need it.

Our mission is to bridge the gap between patients in need and the healthcare resources available to them, saving lives through technology and innovation.
